🎨 Color Thief

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 September 2026 · Made for Kids
The short version Color Thief does not collect, store, or share any personal information — from children or adults. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Everything the game remembers stays on your device.

Who we are

Color Thief is a colouring game for young children (ages 2–6) published by [Your name / studio]. This policy explains what the app does — and doesn't do — with information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for a name, email, photo, location, contacts, or any other personal information, and it does not collect device identifiers. The app makes no network requests of its own; the only connection it uses is Apple's App Store, for purchases (see below).

Information stored on your device

To make the game work, it saves a small amount of data locally on the device only:

  • Game progress — levels completed, stars earned, colours restored
  • Settings — sound effects, music, and vibration on/off
  • Whether the Full Game unlock has been purchased

This data never leaves the device, is not linked to you, and is deleted when the app is deleted.

In-app purchases

Color Thief offers one optional purchase (“Full Game”) that unlocks additional levels. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never see or store payment details. Purchases sit behind a parental gate (a simple math question) so that they can only be completed by a grown-up.

Children's privacy

Color Thief is designed for children and is offered in the Kids category of the App Store. In line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws, we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app contains no third-party software development kits (SDKs), no advertising, no analytics, no social features, and no links to the open web outside the parent-gated settings area (this page).

Device permissions

The app does not request access to the camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts, notifications, or any other protected resource.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes what it does with information, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
